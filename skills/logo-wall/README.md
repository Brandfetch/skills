# Logo wall

Turn a list of brands into a clean logo wall or logo grid.

Use it for a "trusted by" section, a customers or partners slide, an
integrations page, a press strip, or any layout with many company logos.
Claude gets each logo from Brandfetch and sizes them so every logo looks the
same size. You get a PowerPoint slide, PDF, web section or image, on a dark or
light background, in one colour or original brand colours. Claude shows you a
preview before building the final files.

## Ask for it

- "Make a logo grid of our 12 customers"
- "A trusted-by slide with Stripe, Notion and Figma"
- "Our integration partners' logos in white on a dark background"

Or call it by name: `/brandfetch:logo-wall`.

## Get it

Part of the Brandfetch plugin for Claude. [Install it](../../README.md#install)
to get this skill and the Brandfetch MCP.
