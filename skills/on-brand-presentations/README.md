# On-brand presentations

Design a presentation in any company's brand.

Give Claude a brief or an outline and say whose brand to use: yours or another
company's. Claude builds the deck with that brand's logos, colours, fonts and
tone of voice from Brandfetch. Use it for all-hands, roadmaps, training,
investor and board decks, conference talks, and partner decks.

Every fact about a company comes from Brandfetch or from you. When something
is missing, Claude asks instead of guessing.

For a sales deck to a client, use
[`personalized-sales-decks`](../personalized-sales-decks/). For a slide of
customer logos, use [`logo-wall`](../logo-wall/).

## Ask for it

- "Make our all-hands deck in the brandfetch.com brand, from these notes"
- "A Q3 board deck in our brand, using this outline"
- "A training deck on our new pricing, in Acme's brand"
- "A co-branded partner deck for us and Notion"

Or call it by name: `/brandfetch:on-brand-presentations`.

## Get it

Part of the Brandfetch plugin for Claude. [Install it](../../README.md#install)
to get this skill and the Brandfetch MCP.
