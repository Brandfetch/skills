# Personalized sales decks

Turn your sales deck into a ready-to-send deck for any client.

Tell Claude who the deck is for. It fills your PowerPoint template with that
client's logo, colours and company details from Brandfetch, plus the deal
details you give it. Use it for pitches, proposals, QBRs, kickoffs and
renewals.

Set it up once: attach a deck you already sent a client, and Claude turns it
into your template. After that, you name the client and get the deck. No deck
yet? Claude designs one for you.

Every value on a slide comes from Brandfetch or from you. When something is
missing, Claude asks instead of guessing.

## Ask for it

- "Make a sales deck for Stripe"
- "A proposal for Acme"
- "The QBR for Notion"
- "Set up my template", with a deck attached

Or call it by name: `/brandfetch:personalized-sales-decks`.

## Get it

Part of the Brandfetch plugin for Claude. [Install it](../../README.md#install)
to get this skill and the Brandfetch MCP.
